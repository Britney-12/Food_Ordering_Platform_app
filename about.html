About ABUAD Cafeteria Eats

A student project designed to make finding and ordering campus meals more convenient.

Our purpose

Making campus food ordering simpler

ABUAD Cafeteria Eats is a proposed web-based platform that brings cafeteria food options together in one place for students and other members of the university community.

Instead of checking different food spots one by one, users can browse sample menus, select meals, review an order, and view a checkout page. The goal is to make the ordering process easier to understand and more organised.

Browse the menu

Our objectives

  • Display food options from participating campus cafeterias.
  • Help users review their selected meals before checkout.
  • Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
  • Demonstrate how a digital ordering platform could support campus food services.
Our values

Designed around campus needs

Convenience

Make food options easy to browse from one website.

Clarity

Show menu items and prices clearly before users order.

Accessibility

Use a responsive layout that works on phones and computers.

This website is an academic prototype and is not an official ABUAD service unless formally approved by the university.
